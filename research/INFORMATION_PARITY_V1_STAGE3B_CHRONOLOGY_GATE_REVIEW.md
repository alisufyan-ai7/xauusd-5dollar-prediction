## Scientific options (mutually exclusive for the same experiment)

**Option A — preserve the 2026 reserve (status quo, safest scientifically)**

- Keep 2026 unaccessed for historical market fitting, including for Stage 3B.
- Seek an independently sourced expert-decision corpus with source-resolvable timestamps overlapping 2016–2021 TRAIN; only after preregistration and documented provenance could it be joined to accepted TRAIN information.
- Until a viable overlap exists, Stage 3B FULL-vs-GOLD remains NOT TESTED. This is not a profitability failure.
- Benefit: no consumption of 2026 reserve and no retrospective contamination. Limitation: teacher acquisition may prove infeasible or require a different experiment.

**Option B — a separately preregistered bounded 2026 teacher-window exception**

- Explicitly specify a **bounded historical 2026 teacher window** that would cease to qualify as untouched forward/shadow evaluation once used for teacher-fitting.
- Specify disjoint time windows and operational data custody for any 2026 future shadow holdout; the dates used to create teacher labels/features/model cannot later be called pristine forward evaluation.
- Freeze source availability and decision-time rules, precise window, provider instruments/rights, market-feature schema, timezone mapping, missingness, model input parity, embargo rules where necessary, grouping/leave-one-date-out evaluation, and what the 2016–2021 TRAIN model baseline can and cannot contribute.
- Register a gate/decision for allowed provider XAUUSD and synthetic USD context acquisition under the accepted Stage 2 semantics, plus deterministic local validation and controlled CI/provider run budget.
- Require **explicit owner approval and a new written chronology decision BEFORE any source-market joining, reconstruction, or fitting**.
- Risk: consumes a portion of the 2026 reserve and can produce an expert-recognition diagnostic rather than a genuinely forward-prospective outcome.

**Option C — stop this particular teacher-market join**

- Freeze the current source dataset as a scientifically reproducible information asset.
- Do not claim that Stage 3B was executed or that any trading edge was confirmed.
- New teacher-source or information hypotheses would need new preregistration, preserving existing negative/insufficient results without rescue tuning.
